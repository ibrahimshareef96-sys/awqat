Your day, set by the five prayers.

أوقات

Awqat ("the times") is a calm phone app that runs your day on the prayer times. Prayer, Quran, dhikr, study, books, training, food and money live in one place, and the screen changes with the time of day. After Fajr it opens on the morning adhkar. On Friday it asks about Al-Kahf. After Isha it brings up what you set yourself to study.

What it does

Prayer

Times worked out on the phone for where you are, the iqama with your mosque's minutes, a reminder at each prayer, and a log of what you prayed, with the prayers you owe.

Quran

The Madinah mushaf, all 604 pages, with notes, a bookmark that follows your reading, a daily goal and a revision plan by juz. Downloadable for offline reading.

Recite to find

Recite three or four words and the page opens with the ayah lit. Memorise mode hides a page and fills it in as you recite. The speech model runs on the phone.

Adhkar

The adhkar after the salam, morning and evening, with a tap ring for each count, and how to say each one and what it means in English.

Training and food

A plan for each day, a workout timer on the lock screen, your lifts with what to try next. Meals by text, photo or barcode, against goals you set.

Weekly review

A muhasabah in four short steps: the week's numbers, three questions, up to three intentions, and the week ahead. Never one score.

Study and books

Tafsir, Hadith and Fiqh tracks with a weekly target and a timer, and a reading list with a barcode scanner and a Goodreads import.

Widgets and voice

The next prayer, the Hijri date, today's page and the adhkar on your home and lock screen. Siri phrases on iPhone, shortcuts and a tile on Android.

Your records stay on your phone

No account. No server of ours. No analytics. No ads. Awqat reaches public services only for what you ask of it, like the mushaf's fonts or a book's cover. Your recitation is heard on the phone and sent nowhere. No streaks, no scores, no guilt.
