Awqat and your privacy

Awqat keeps your records on your phone. It has no account, no server of its own, no analytics and no ads. This page says what it reaches over the network, and what it does with your health data.

What it reaches, and when

  • Quran Foundation, when you open a mushaf page: that page's font and words. The request says which page.
  • Open Library, when you scan or look up a book: the ISBN or title you asked about.
  • The calendar addresses you add, to read your calendars.
  • Your location stays on the phone, rounded to about a kilometre, unless you use Talk: then it is sent, rounded, to Open-Meteo for the weather at the start of each talk.

Two things only happen if you set them up yourself with your own server. Sync keeps your records (prayers, Quran, adhkar, learning, books, body, money and your weekly reviews) in step between your devices through that server. Talk, the voice assistant, sends your voice to ElevenLabs while a talk is on, and tells the assistant about your day, including the intentions you set for the week; your money and your health are included only if you turn them on in Settings.

Your health data: what it reads

  • Weight, over the last 30 days.
  • Steps, by day, over the last 7 days.
  • Sleep sessions, over the last 7 nights.
  • Workouts (exercise sessions), from Monday of this week.

It reads only what you allow in Health Connect or Apple Health, and you can take any of it back there at any time.

Your health data: what it keeps

The numbers are read when you open Body and shown on the screen. One is kept: today's step count, as a line for the home-screen widget, in the widget's own storage on this phone. Weights you type into the app yourself, and your weekly workout plan, are kept inside the app on this phone.

Your health data: where it can go

Out of the box, nowhere. With your own sync server set up, the weights you typed and your workout plan are kept in step through it. With Talk set up and "Health in talks" turned on in Settings, your steps and last night's sleep are passed to the voice service for that talk. It is off until you turn it on.

What it never does

  • It never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health.
  • It never shares, sells or shows your health data to anyone, and it has no advertising or analytics.
