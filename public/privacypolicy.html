Awqat and your privacy

Awqat keeps your records on your phone. It has no account, no server of its own, no analytics and no ads. This page says what it reaches over the network, and what it does with your health data.

What it reaches, and when

  • Quran Foundation, when you open a mushaf page: that page's font and words. The request says which page.
  • hisnmuslim.com and Quran Foundation, when you open the adhkar or the dhikr after a prayer with Sound or Meaning on: the English and the transliteration. The request says which chapter or which ayat. It is made when nothing is kept on the phone yet, then about once a week; a request that failed is made again after ten minutes. Like any website, these services see your phone's internet address. Turn Sound and Meaning off and nothing is asked.
  • Hugging Face and the Tanzil Project, once, when you first tap Download to recite to find an ayah or memorise a page: the Quran speech model and the Quran text, about 45 MB. Both stay on the phone until you remove them in Settings. What you recite is heard on the phone by that model and never sent anywhere. The microphone is on only while the screen says it is listening; sending the app to the background stops it.
  • Open Library, when you scan or look up a book: the ISBN or title you asked about.
  • Open Food Facts, when you scan a packet in Food: the digits of its barcode, and nothing else the app knows about you or what you ate. Like any website, it sees your phone's internet address.
  • The calendar addresses you add, to read your calendars.
  • Your location stays on the phone, rounded to about a kilometre, unless you use Talk: then it is sent, rounded, to Open-Meteo for the weather at the start of each talk.

Two things only happen if you set them up yourself with your own server. Sync keeps your records (prayers, Quran, adhkar, learning, books, body, food, money and your weekly reviews) in step between your devices through that server; a meal's photo never syncs. With that server, tapping "Estimate from the photo" sends that one photo to it. Talk, the voice assistant, sends your voice to ElevenLabs while a talk is on, and tells the assistant about your day, including the intentions you set for the week; your money and your health are included only if you turn them on in Settings.

Your meals and their photos

What you log in Food (meals, their numbers and your goals) is kept on this phone. A photo of a meal is redrawn without the camera's location and details, kept in the app's own folder and not your gallery, and deleted when you remove the meal on this phone or clear the form. A meal removed on another of your devices leaves its photo here for a day or two, until the app next starts and clears it. On an iPhone or iPad the photos are part of your own device backup, like the rest of the app's records. No photo is sent anywhere unless you run your own server and tap Estimate on it.

What the widgets keep

The home-screen widgets work with the app closed, so the app copies what they show into their own storage on this phone: the coming prayer times, your Quran page and today's reading, whether the adhkar are said, and the titles and times of today's and tomorrow's meetings. Meeting titles show only on the home screen, never on the lock screen. Siri's answer to "When is the next prayer in Awqat" is read from the same copy.

Your health data: what it reads

  • Weight, over the last 30 days.
  • Steps, by day, over the last 7 days.
  • Sleep sessions, over the last 7 nights.
  • Workouts (exercise sessions), from Monday of this week.

It reads only what you allow in Health Connect or Apple Health, and you can take any of it back there at any time.

Your health data: what it keeps

The numbers are read when you open Body and shown on the screen. One is kept: today's step count, as a line for the home-screen widget, in the widget's own storage on this phone. Weights you type into the app yourself, and your weekly workout plan, are kept inside the app on this phone.

Your health data: where it can go

Out of the box, nowhere. With your own sync server set up, the weights you typed and your workout plan are kept in step through it. With Talk set up and "Health in talks" turned on in Settings, your steps and last night's sleep are passed to the voice service for that talk. It is off until you turn it on.

What it never does

  • It never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health.
  • It never shares, sells or shows your health data to anyone, and it has no advertising or analytics.
