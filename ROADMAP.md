# Roadmap

Ranked by what they give a Muslim user against what they cost to build. Everything here works offline and needs no
server unless it says so. Nothing here types or generates Quran text.

## Next

1. **Back up and restore everything** (S to M). One file of all your records, exported and imported through the
   phone's share sheet, optionally encrypted with a passphrase. Today the only way to keep data across phones is the
   optional sync server.
2. **Asr by madhhab, and minute adjustments per prayer** (M). Hanafi Asr (adhan-js supports it) and a plus or minus
   for each time, to match a local mosque's timetable.
3. **Hijri date adjustment** (S). A day forward or back to follow a local moon sighting.
4. **Qibla** (M). The bearing from your place, offline, with the compass where the phone has one.
5. **Keep the screen awake while reading the mushaf** (S).

## After that

6. **Fasting** (M): Ramadan, make-up days and voluntary fasts (Mondays and Thursdays, the white days), with optional
   reminders and a line in the Weekly Review.
7. **Excused days and make-up per prayer** (M), so the week's summary is never misleading.
8. **The last third of the night** (S): its start on Faith, and an optional reminder.
9. **A free tasbih** (S): the counter ring for any dhikr you choose.
10. **Hijri date and fasting on the widgets** (S).
11. **Money in any currency** (M): today it adds up in AED, with USD converted.
12. **A reference sync and voice server** (L): an open version of the backend in docs/backend.md, with the meal
    estimate route in docs/food.md.
13. **Training programmes** (M): a plan of exercises with sets and reps for each day, ticked off as you go, instead of
    one line a day.
14. **Food you eat often** (S): one tap to log a meal you logged before.
15. **Water, and fasting days in Food** (S): a day you fast shows as a fast, not as a day with nothing logged.

## Never

- Typing, generating or bundling Quran text.
- Ads, analytics, or anything that sends your records to a server you did not set up.
- Streaks, scores or nudges that turn worship into a game.
